# Physical iPhone acceptance

Use iOS 17+ with a Turkish voice available. These checks need a real iPhone and have not been claimed as executed on Windows.

- Cold launch; onboard; relaunch and confirm profile persists.
- Open each lesson audio library and play every word/example/phrase/dialogue line. Confirm Turkish voice and stop behavior.
- First speaking task: confirm both permission dialogs appear. Deny each separately, retry from Settings, and confirm no false success or blocked permission request after accepting.
- Record the target phrase, stop, replay your recording, wait for recognized text and comparison. Try an incorrect phrase, silence, a too-short clip, a reordered sentence, and a valid phrase.
- Record until 45 seconds. Background while recording or recognizing, return, retry. Disconnect headphones and receive an interruption. Confirm microphone stops on background and exercise exit.
- Disable internet on devices with and without on-device Turkish recognition. Confirm a truthful retry state where unavailable.
- Switch exercises while speech is active and confirm old callbacks cannot credit the new exercise.
- Confirm Capi changes for all twelve contexts: onboarding welcome/profile, reading/learning, listening, speaking, processing/thinking, correct/retry, café/travel/city, final celebration.
- Toggle sounds and haptics, test with silent mode and headphones. Turn Reduce Motion on and verify no continuous mascot/confetti animation.
- Complete a lesson, relaunch, check sequential unlock and saved progress. Replaying an unfinished recording must not count as passing speech recognition.
- Test small iPhone size, large text, VoiceOver labels, keyboard entry and navigation.
